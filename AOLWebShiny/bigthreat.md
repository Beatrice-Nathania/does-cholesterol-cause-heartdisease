## How **Big** Is the Heart Disease Problem? Let the Numbers Speak

Heart and circulatory diseases cause nearly **1 in 3 deaths globally**; an estimated *20 million deaths in 2021* -
an average of 55,000 people each day or one death every 1.5 seconds. They are the world’s biggest killers.
More than four out of five hearth disease deaths are due to heart attacks, and one third of these deaths occur prematurely in people under 70 years of age.
