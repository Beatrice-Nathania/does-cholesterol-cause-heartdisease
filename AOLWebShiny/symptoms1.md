There are some common symptoms if someone got a heart disease, and those are :
1. Chest pain or discomfort : Often described as pressure, tightness, or squeezing.
2. Dizziness : Feeling faint or dizzy, especially when standing up. 
3. Fatigue : Unusual tiredness or weakness. 
4. Racing heartbeat : An arrhythmia can cause a racing, fluttering, or slow heartbeat. 
5. Shortness of breath : Can occur during activity, while resting, or when lying flat.
6. Slow heartbeat : often caused by problems with the heart's electrical system not functioning properly or disruptions in the heart's conduction pathways.
