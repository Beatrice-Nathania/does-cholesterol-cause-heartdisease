## What is Heart Disease?

Heart disease encompasses a variety of conditions that affect the heart's structure and function, impacting blood flow and the heart's ability to pump effectively. 
Heart disease are also known as Cardiovascular Disease. Heart disease is a broad term that includes conditions like coronary artery disease, arrhythmias, heart failure and others. 


Here is a brief explanation of the different heart disease conditions :
1. Coronary Artery Disease (CAD):
   This is the most common type of heart disease, where plaque buildup narrows or blocks the arteries that supply blood to the heart, potentially leading to chest pain or heart attack. 
2. Arrhythmias:
   These are irregular heart rhythms, which can range from harmless to life-threatening. 
3. Heart Failure:
   This occurs when the heart can't pump enough blood to meet the body's needs, often due to weakened heart muscle or damaged valves.
