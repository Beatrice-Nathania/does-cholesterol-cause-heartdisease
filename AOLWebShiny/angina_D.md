#1. Angina or Chest Pain after Exercise
Most patient got angina after exercise as one of the symptoms.
Angina, or chest pain that worsens with physical activity or stress, is a common symptom of heart disease, specifically coronary artery disease (CAD).
Angina occurs when the heart muscle doesn't get enough oxygen because the blood vessels supplying it are narrowed or blocked. 
Angina is often triggered by activities that increase the heart's demand for oxygen, like exercise, stress, or even cold weather.
