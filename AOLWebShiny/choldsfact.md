### The data shows that there are more than **500 patient** that suffered from ***high cholesterol***.
High cholesterol, also known as hypercholesterolemia, refers to having an excessive amount of cholesterol in the blood. 
Cholesterol is a waxy, fat-like substance that the body needs to build healthy cells.

Cholesterol are measured by total cholesterol levels in milligrams per deciliter (mg/dL). 
A total cholesterol level of 240 mg/dL or higher is considered high.

From this result, we analyze how many percentage of patient with high Cholesterol that *had heart disease*.
