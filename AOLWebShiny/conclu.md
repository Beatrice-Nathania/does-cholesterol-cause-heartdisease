The Sustainable Development Goal, adopted by all United Nations Member States in 2015, provides a shared blueprint for peace and prosperity for people and the planet, now and into the future. At its heart are the 17 Sustainable Development Goals (SDGs), which are an urgent call for action by all countries - developed and developing - in a global partnership.
Based on Goal 3, Good Health and well being are adopted to ensure healthy lives and promote well being for all ages. One of its sub-target is Goal 3.4.1 Mortality rate attributed to cardiovascular disease, cancer, diabetes or chronic disease in hope to reduce premature mortality from those diseases by 2030.

Heart disease are the leading cause of Non communicable disease related premature death globally. Since SDG 3.4.1 targets reduction of deaths from NCDs, addressing heart disease is a critical component.
Heart disease preventive efforts are encouraged by sdg 3.4.1

From the patient data, we can conclude that ***cholesterol does cause heart disease***. 
People with high cholesterol have an increased the risk of having a heart disease. Cholesterol **is an important** factor that cause heart disease.

But it’s not enough to look at cholesterol alone. Heart disease is complex. 
There are other factor of heart disease like, age, blood pressure, blood sugar, and more that shouldn't overlook.

Apart from the risk factor, it is also important to identify the symptoms of heart disease that an individual have. By detecting and treating heart conditions early this can reduce the risk of premature death
